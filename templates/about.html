About Aruvon

Bridging academics and employability with real-world learning.

Aruvon Learning Solutions is a skill development and placement training institute working directly with colleges to make students placement-ready — through expert trainers, industry-aligned curriculum, and a focus on career readiness.

Growing across Tamil Nadu, one batch at a time.

  • Successfully trained 250+ students across Tamil Nadu
  • Delivered sessions on Aptitude, Communication, and Industry Tools (like Salesforce, ROS 2, and more)
  • Partnered with Engineering and Arts colleges for workshops
  • Expanding Salesforce partnerships and skill initiatives
